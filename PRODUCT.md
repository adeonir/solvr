# Product: Solvr

## Register

`product`. Solvr serves a task, which is calculating in text. The experience exists to give a result and keep the work.

## Users

Solvr is for people on the Mac who calculate in text every day, starting with a developer who reaches for it many times during work. Solvr relates to them as a discreet tool: it stays out of the way, answers when they type, and keeps their sheets ready for the next time.

## Product Purpose

Solvr is a text calculator for the Mac that combines a calculation language with sheets that stay saved, organized, and synced through iCloud. A sheet in Solvr is a place to come back to, where earlier lines and variables keep their meaning.

## Brand Personality

Discreet, fast, and precise. The tone is direct and uses few words, with no decoration. A result appears without asking for attention.

## Anti-references

Solvr is not a calculator for a single calculation that forgets the work after the answer. Solvr keeps sheets and remembers earlier calculations until someone deletes them. Solvr does not compete with launchers such as Raycast and Spotlight.

## Design Principles

The text is the interface: what the user types is the sheet, with no form or button in the way. The result does not ask for attention: it appears next to its line while the user types. The sheet keeps the context: variables and earlier lines stay valid until someone deletes them.
