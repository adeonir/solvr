# Intent: Organize sheets in folders

## Problem

The calculators the author uses cannot group sheets. The evidence comes from personal daily use.

## Proposed outcome

Users can group sheets in named folders, move sheets into and out of folders, rename folders, and delete folders.

## Affected users and systems

The author and other Mac users with the same use cases; sheets and folders.

## Constraints

- A sheet belongs to at most one folder and can belong to no folder.
- Deleting a folder deletes its sheets. Only the first folder deletion asks for confirmation.

## Open questions

None.
